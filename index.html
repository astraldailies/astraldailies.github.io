How we publish

We publish our own videos to our own channels and pages. Our in-house publishing tool connects to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram through their official APIs (YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API) only to upload and manage videos on accounts we own. We do not access, collect or process data of other users. See our Privacy Policy.
