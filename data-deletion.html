Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 9 October 2026

The Astral Dailies App only connects to accounts owned by Astral Dailies and does not store data about other people. If you believe we hold any data about you, or you want data linked to your account removed:

  1. Email astraldailies@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the account or page name concerned.
  2. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

Remove the App's access yourself

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations > Astral Dailies > Remove.
  • Instagram: Settings > Apps and websites > Astral Dailies > Remove.
  • Google / YouTube: myaccount.google.com/permissions > Astral Dailies > Remove access.

When access is removed, any tokens we held for that account stop working and are deleted from our system.
