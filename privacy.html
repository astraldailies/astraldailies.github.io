Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Astral Dailies ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and our in-house publishing application, also named Astral Dailies (the "App"). The App is used only by Astral Dailies to publish and manage videos on YouTube channels, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that we own.

1. Who we are

Astral Dailies, United Arab Emirates. Contact: astraldailies@gmail.com.

2. Information the App accesses

When we connect our own accounts, the App receives, through the platforms' official sign-in (OAuth):

  • Google / YouTube: an access token and refresh token for our YouTube channel, the channel's ID and name, and the IDs and status of videos we upload.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): access tokens for our Facebook Pages and linked Instagram professional accounts, the Page and account IDs and names, and the IDs and status of posts we publish.

The App does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or other users, such as their profiles, comments, messages or contacts.

3. How we use it

  • To upload our videos with their titles, descriptions and tags, and to publish, update, make private or delete our own posts.
  • To read aggregate performance statistics of our own channels and pages (for example views) to plan our content.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on our own computer, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and are never sent to any server other than the platform they belong to. Access is limited to Astral Dailies staff.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected and are deleted when we disconnect it. You can ask us to delete any data at any time; see Data deletion.

6. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Meta platforms

The App uses the Meta Graph API in line with the Meta Platform Terms. Access can be removed at any time in Facebook Settings > Business Integrations, or in Instagram Settings > Apps and websites.

8. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect any information from visitors.

9. Children

Our App is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact

Questions or requests: astraldailies@gmail.com.
